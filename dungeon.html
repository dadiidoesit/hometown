WHAT YOUR BOOKING INCLUDES

You're not paying for somebody to read a module aloud. You're hiring a narrator, organizer, improviser, and referee to do the preparation so your group can actually play.

  • Consistency: a scheduled, prepared session with clear communication and ongoing continuity. I have completed more than ten campaigns; I'm interested in finishing the story, not vanishing after the tavern scene.
  • Your characters matter: backstories, ambitions, relationships, and questionable choices influence the world and its inhabitants.
  • Professional creative work: custom NPCs, voices, handouts, maps, tokens, and music when they improve the experience.
  • A welcoming, well-run table: attentive group facilitation, boundaries discussed ahead of time, and time in the spotlight for everybody.
  • Continuity: written recaps for ongoing games, so absences and real life don't erase the campaign's history.
  • Help for beginners: an introduction to the rules you actually need, without a six-hour lecture about armor class.

Before booking: send a brief message about your group and what you'd like to play. I'll help match the format to your players, interests, schedule, and budget. There's no need to select the most expensive option to ask a question.

ABOUT THE GAMES

I have been roleplaying for nearly twenty years and running games for more than ten. I began in play-by-post forums and IRC channels, where I learned that a good story doesn't require expensive miniatures, elaborate mechanics, or even a particularly good plot. Sometimes all you need is a few interesting people willing to take each other seriously for a couple of hours.

Outside the game, I've worked as a writer, illustrator, educator, and coordinator of collaborative projects. I know how to listen, help people find a shared direction, and keep a project moving without taking ownership away from the people involved. That turns out to be useful when six adventurers are trying to agree on how to get through a locked door.

My games are character-driven. Player choices matter. I like worlds that respond to what people do, rather than railroads that push them through a predetermined story or empty sandboxes where everyone waits for something interesting to happen.

I enjoy dramatic dialogue, mysteries, personal rivalries, unlikely friendships, and cinematic combat. I use maps and tokens when they help. I don't believe every conversation needs a battle map or every battle needs to become a three-hour accounting exercise.

I like the Rule of Cool. I also like rules. These positions are not mutually exclusive. I want everyone at the table to feel included, accomplished, and excited to come back. A professional game should be organized without feeling scripted, and welcoming without asking anybody to leave their imagination at the door.

I am a published writer, artist, educator, and voice actor. I create original settings, maps, NPCs, tokens, handouts, and campaign documentation. NPCs frequently have their own voices. Some of them should not.

PLAYERS WANTED / ALL EXPERIENCE LEVELS

Beginners are very welcome. I enjoy teaching people the nuts and bolts of a game so they can get to the part they actually came for: playing a character and telling a story. You don't need to memorize THAC0 to have a good time. You also don't need to show up with an elaborate character biography. We'll find a place to start.

Experienced players and rules enthusiasts are welcome too. I can run a more rules-faithful table when that's what the group prefers. Friendly rules discussion is fine; my ruling stands during play so we can keep moving.

I enjoy players who inhabit their characters, make bold choices, collaborate, and take an interest in the other people at the table. Different playstyles are fine. Please be considerate of other players, their boundaries, and their time. A little structure goes a long way toward letting everybody relax and be ridiculous together.

Harassment and unwanted behavior are not tolerated. We're here to have a good time together.

A PERSONAL NOTE / SEEKING GROUP

Although this is a professional advertisement, I'm also interested in finding a regular group to play with for the simple pleasure of playing.

My first games were little windows of text, with people scattered around the world typing to one another late into the night. Sometimes a single conversation took an evening. Sometimes somebody wrote one paragraph that changed everything, and everybody suddenly remembered why they were there.

I still like that kind of game.

The campaigns I remember best aren't necessarily the ones with the most elaborate plots or impressive battles. They're the ones where the players became friends, the characters developed histories, and the world started to feel like somewhere we'd actually been. A little community grows around a table like that, provided people give it enough time.

I've run a five-year campaign. I'd like to run a thirty-year campaign.

I realize that is an unusual thing to put in a classified advertisement.

What I mean is that I'm especially interested in a group that enjoys playing together enough to keep doing it. A group that develops its own traditions. That remembers old characters. That can laugh about something that happened ten years ago and immediately begin arguing about whose fault it was.

I don't expect every game to become that. I don't even expect most of them to. But I think it's worth looking.

If you have a friendly, committed, imaginative group and a story you'd like to tell, please get in touch. Tell me who you are, what you enjoy, and what kind of game you've always wanted to play. I'm open to recreational games with the right people.

In the meantime, I am available for paid engagements, special events, short adventures, and ordinary Thursday evenings.
